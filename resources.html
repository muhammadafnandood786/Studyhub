Resources & Study Guides

Students ke liye practical tips, useful links aur subject-wise guidance.

Top Study Tips

  • Active Recall: Notes dubara padhne ke bajaye khud se yaad karne ki koshish karo.
  • Spaced Repetition: Ek hi cheez ko alag-alag dinon mein revise karo — long-term memory ke liye best.
  • Time Blocking: Har subject ke liye fixed time slot rakho. Multitasking avoid karo.
  • Sleep & Breaks: 7–8 hours neend aur short breaks productivity badhate hain.
  • Teach Someone: Jo seekha hai kisi ko samjhao — understanding deep hoti hai.

Exam Prep Checklist

  1. Syllabus complete kar lo aur weak topics identify karo
  2. Past papers solve karo (time-bound)
  3. Short notes / flashcards banaye
  4. Sleep schedule fix rakho exam se 1 week pehle
  5. Exam day: water, light breakfast, calm mindset
